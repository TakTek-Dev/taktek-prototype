01What we do

Eight disciplines, three practices, one team.

Most projects need more than one of these. That is the point: the people who design your interface also write its code and keep it running.

01

Build

Websites, apps and software, designed and coded by the same team.

  • Websites

    News sites, platforms and company sites in Arabic, English, Turkish or all three, with a control panel your team can use from day one.

  • Mobile apps

    Apps for iOS and Android that share content and accounts with your website instead of living beside it.

  • Custom software

    Dashboards, portals and internal tools shaped around how your team works. Our own client portal and Devlo are both examples.

Read the practice: Build
02

Run

Hosting, updates and support for what we build, with every request in writing.

  • Hosting and servers

    We host and deploy what we build. Updates reach every site we run through one deployment process, and we check their speed as part of the routine.

  • Support and care

    A free support period after launch, then maintenance and development plans. Tickets, invoices and progress live in the client portal.

Read the practice: Run
03

Brand and media

Identity, graphics, marketing and video that look like they belong to the product.

  • Visual identity

    Logo, color, type and rules that hold up in Arabic and Latin script, on screens and in print.

  • Graphic design

    Social cards, campaign visuals and editorial graphics produced inside your identity, not around it.

  • Digital marketing

    Getting the right people to what we built: search, social channels and campaigns measured against goals agreed up front.

  • Video and editing

    Editing, motion graphics and titles for news, programmes and campaigns, cut for each platform they run on.

05Questions

What clients ask before we start.

How long does a website or app take?

It depends on the size. Company sites usually take 2–4 weeks; apps and custom systems 6–12 weeks. You get a clear timeline on day one.

Can I manage the site myself after launch?

Yes. Every site ships with a control panel where you edit content and preview it before publishing, without code. Newsrooms get Devlo.

How is a project priced?

After a short introductory call we send a written proposal with scope, cost and duration. No surprises later.

Do you support the project after launch?

Yes. Every project includes a free support period, followed by maintenance and development plans if you want them.

Do you work with clients in other countries?

Yes. We work with clients in several countries, and all communication and follow-up happen online.
