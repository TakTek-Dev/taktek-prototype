Brand and media

Identity, graphics, marketing and video that look like they belong to the product.

A product needs a face and a voice that carry from the screen to a social feed to a video. We design the system once and apply it everywhere, in every language you publish in.

  • Visual identity

    Logo, color, type and rules that hold up in Arabic and Latin script, on screens and in print.

    What it covers

    • Logo in Arabic and Latin
    • Color and type system
    • Brand guidelines
    • Templates for daily use

  • Graphic design

    Social cards, campaign visuals and editorial graphics produced inside your identity, not around it.

    What it covers

    • Social media templates
    • Campaign visuals
    • Infographics
    • Print material

  • Digital marketing

    Getting the right people to what we built: search, social channels and campaigns measured against goals agreed up front.

    What it covers

    • Search optimisation
    • Social channel setup
    • Campaign planning
    • Reporting against agreed goals

  • Video and editing

    Editing, motion graphics and titles for news, programmes and campaigns, cut for each platform they run on.

    What it covers

    • Editing
    • Motion graphics
    • Titles and lower thirds
    • Formats for each platform
