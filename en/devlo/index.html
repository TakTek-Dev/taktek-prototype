Your newsroom, on air.

The CMS built around how a news desk actually works.

Breaking news under pressure, live coverage, wire-style intake from Telegram and RSS, and the same story in three languages. Devlo runs it from one panel that starts in Arabic, and each newsroom keeps its own site, domain and data.

The rundown

From the wire to the page, in four stages.

Each stage below is a set of Devlo modules. Your newsroom switches on the ones it uses; the panel shows nothing else.

  1. 06:00Standby

    Intake

    Stories arrive before anyone writes them.

    • Import from Telegram channels, a Telegram bot and RSS feeds into an inbox Add-on
    • Optional rewriting of imported items in your house style Add-on
    • Reader submissions and contact messages
  2. 08:30Draft

    Desk

    Write, check, schedule, hand over.

    • Editor with drafts, a publishing calendar and pending actions
    • AI assistant: editing tools, image descriptions, a one-minute summary and SEO fixes to review Add-on
    • Dossiers, quotes, polls and events
    • One media library with bulk upload for images, video, audio and podcasts
  3. 12:04On air

    On air

    Breaking, live and out to your channels.

    • Breaking-news ticker with a web push to readers
    • Live coverage with a live desk; the story page updates itself
    • Posting stories and breaking news to your own social channels Add-on
    • Live streams on the site
  4. 18:00Published

    Reach

    Found, shared, read again.

    • SEO tools, structured data and sitemaps
    • Share cards checked for WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook and X
    • Newsletters and reader membership plans
    • Analytics dashboard with a downloadable PDF report

Add-on Marked modules are paid add-ons. Everything is enabled per newsroom, so a site only carries what it uses.

Editions

Arabic first. English and Turkish as equals.

The panel is written in Arabic, English and Turkish, right to left by default. A site can publish several editions; each story knows its translations, and a story opened in the wrong edition takes you to the one it lives in.

  • Automatic translation into your other editions, with review before publishing or straight to air
  • Sections and tags linked to their counterparts in each language
  • A daily translation limit you control
  • Each story's translations shown beside it in the list

Ownership

One codebase, many newsrooms, nothing shared between them.

Every Devlo newsroom runs on its own domain with its own database and its own front end, designed for it by TakTek. A newsroom never sees another's name, content or data. Updates reach all of them through one release process.

Control

Who can do what, down to each action.

  • Permissions per module and per action: view, create, edit, publish, delete
  • Two-factor sign-in, rate-limited login
  • Activity log of every account
  • A command palette and guided tours for new editors

Moving in

Bring your archive, and your desk's passwords.

Devlo imports from WordPress and from other systems: stories, media, authors and dates. Your editors' WordPress accounts can come over with their existing passwords, and old links keep working through redirects.
