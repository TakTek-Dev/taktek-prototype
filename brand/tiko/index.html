Brand guidelines / Tiko

Tiko, redrawn

Tiko is TakTek's assistant in the client portal, redrawn in the TakTek identity:

  • a soft tile with real volume;
  • the wordmark's lime dot floating above him, in place of an antenna;
  • the wordmark's dot pair, split in two, for eyes;
  • a small smile.

This board records how he got here, then sets out his forms, states and motion as they now ship in the portal and on the site.

The Tiko we started from

  • A friendly robot the company liked: antenna, ear discs, a visor with lime eyes and a smile. His warmth and his smile are what this redesign keeps.
  • It was built from the old navy palette, not from the wordmark, with seven gradients, two blur filters and a glow under him in every instance. On the website its idle loops cost about half a second of blocking time.
  • Fifteen moods, three of them with no job in the app; hearts, sweat drops and sparkles.
  • At 18 to 28 px, the size he mostly appears at in the chat, the visor and smile blurred into a lime smudge.

How he got here

First pass: the tile

  • Tiko became a flat ink tile whose eyes were the wordmark's dot pair.
  • It was on brand and light, and it introduced the moves he keeps today: the tak-tek, the fusion into the wordmark's dots, the eyes reading along an answer.
  • But it lost the character the company knew, and the owner did not take to it.

Second pass: three directions

  • A: the old silhouette, redrawn in the identity.
  • B: the tile given volume, a lime dot for an antenna and a small mouth.
  • C: a tile with a visor.
  • The owner chose B on 26 October 2026, in the dark colourway on the left. Everything the first pass had learned to do carried over to him.

What the identity gives him

The TakThe wordmark's dot pair: two rounded diamonds joined at a soft waist. Split in two, they are his eyes; one dot floats above him for an antenna.

Ink, pearl and limeAn ink tile with lime eyes on light surfaces; a pearl tile with ink eyes on dark ones, as the old Tiko had. The lime is the wordmark's own, #DFFF00.

The clickcubic-bezier(.3, 1.45, .55, 1): arrives, overshoots a hair, settles. Every move of his uses it.

Forms, colourways and sizes

Ink on light surfaces and pearl on dark ones. In the portal the colourway follows the theme on its own. At 32 px and under he is the tile alone: the dot above him drops away so the eyes and smile fill the space.

States

One state for each thing the assistant actually does. The code name under each is what the portal sets.

Idle

Nothing is happening. He blinks, sometimes twice. Where he can be pointed at he turns towards the cursor, the head a little and the eyes further; left alone a while, his eyes give one tak-tek, like a clock.

default

Hello

The chat opens, a conversation is cleared, or he says hello once per visit. Smiling eyes, a wider smile, a nod, and the dot above him bounces.

wave

Listening

Someone is typing to him. Curious: the head tilts, one eye opens wider, the eyes drop to the field, and each burst of typing gets a small nod.

listening

Thinking

A question or an action is with the server. The eyes look up and tick a quarter turn in turn, tak, tek; the dot above him keeps time.

thinking

Answering

His answer is typing out. His eyes read along the line, right to left in Arabic and left to right in English, and his mouth talks.

talking

Done well

A helper finished: a draft written, a proposal sent. Smiling eyes and a hop.

happy

Warm

A tip he likes to share on the dashboard. Smiling eyes and a held tilt of the head.

love

Done

A ticket or budget lines were created from the chat. The eyes fuse into the wordmark's dots.

celebrate

Sorry

A request failed or hit the rate limit. The eyes slant, the dot sinks, he bows; the message itself says what happened.

sorry

Look here

On a dashboard he points to the item he is talking about, towards the end of the line.

point

Oh

Something unusual on the dashboard, or waking up. Wide eyes, an open mouth, and the dot jumps.

surprised

Resting

Twenty quiet seconds on the launcher or the dashboard. Eyes closed, the dot settled lower. Any movement wakes him.

sleep

Tap

A tap on him: the tile presses. Three quick taps fuse the eyes.

giggle, dizzy

Motion

Quiet by default: at rest he only blinks. Every move is a transform on the SVG, never a filter, so a page of chat avatars costs nothing.

Using him

Do

  • Let the state tell the truth: thinking only while a request is out, sorry only beside an error message.
  • Pair him with words. His face never replaces a message or a label.
  • Ink on light, pearl on dark.

Don't

  • Add props, limbs, ears, a glow or a shadow halo beneath him.
  • Recolour the lime, or set him on a busy gradient.
  • Stretch him, or move him beyond his own moves.

Sizes

  • Launcher 56–64 px, chat header 40 px, dashboard 96–184 px: the whole figure.
  • Messages and inline labels, 18–28 px: the tile alone.
  • He keeps a clear space of a sixth of his size.
