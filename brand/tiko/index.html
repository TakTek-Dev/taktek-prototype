Brand guidelines / Tiko

Tiko, redrawn from the wordmark

Tiko is TakTek's assistant in the client portal. This board records the redesign: what the old character was, what the new identity gives him, three directions, the one chosen, and the finished system that now ships in the portal and on the site.

The Tiko we started from

  • A toy robot: antenna, ear discs, visor, gloss and a glow underneath. Built from the old navy palette, not from the wordmark.
  • Fifteen moods, three of them (love, dizzy, four random celebrations) with no job in the app. Hearts, sweat drops, sparkles and «zzz» set in Cairo.
  • Seven gradients and two blur filters per instance; the idle loops repainted blurred SVG every frame. On the website that cost about half a second of blocking time.
  • At 18 to 28 px, the size he mostly appears at in the chat, the visor and smile blur into a lime smudge.
  • What worked, and is kept: the name, his voice, lime eyes on a dark face, and the idea that his face tells you what the assistant is doing.

What the identity gives him

Every part of the new Tiko is already in the TakTek identity. Nothing is borrowed from mascot kits.

The TakThe wordmark's dot pair: two rounded diamonds joined at a soft waist. Split in two, they are his eyes.

The ink tileThe app icon's tile. Tiko's head is its sibling, with a continuous corner.

Ink and signalInk #101413 and the wordmark's lime #DFFF00, at 15:1. No gradients, no glow.

The clickcubic-bezier(.3, 1.45, .55, 1): arrives, overshoots a hair, settles. Every move of his uses it.

Three directions

Each drawn on white and on ink, at work size and at the 24 px he mostly lives at in the chat.

A · The Tak pair

Abstract brand intelligence

No head, no face: Tiko is the dot pair itself. He listens, thinks and answers through how the two diamonds move: they part, turn, orbit and, when the work is done, fuse into the wordmark's dots.

B · The Tile

Refined digital companion · chosen

An ink tile, sibling of the app icon, whose eyes are the Tak split in two. Enough of a face to be someone you are talking to, nothing more: no mouth, no limbs, no props. A's best idea lives inside it: on success the eyes slide together and become the wordmark's dots.

C · The Lattice cell

Signature interactive entity

One cell of the homepage lattice, come alive: a diamond head holding a matrix of small diamonds that light up in patterns, eyes, a running light, a check.

A is the purest mark but, without a face, it reads as a loading indicator in a chat, and sorry or listening have no honest form. C reads as a status display, and its matrix turns to noise at 24 px. B keeps a face the eye finds at any size, every state has a form, and it carries A's fusion as its success moment.

Forms, colourways and sizes

Two colourways. Ink on any light or navy surface; Signal (lime tile, ink eyes) only on ink, such as the chat header and the site's hero. At 30 px and below the compact form draws the eyes 12% larger and closer, so they stay two clear diamonds. A hairline rim keeps the ink tile's edge on the portal's dark navy.

States

One state for each thing the assistant actually does. The code name under each is what the portal sets; the old moods with no job map onto the nearest real one.

Idle

Nothing is happening. He blinks now and then; where he can be pointed at, his eyes follow the cursor.

default

Hello

The chat opens, a conversation is cleared, or he says hello once per visit.

wave

Listening

Someone is typing to him. The eyes drop to the field.

listening

Thinking

A question or an action is with the server. The eyes tick a quarter turn in turn: tak, tek.

thinking

Answering

His answer is typing out. A soft cadence, nothing more.

talking

Done well

A helper finished: a draft written, a proposal sent.

happy, love

Done

A ticket or budget lines were created from the chat. The eyes fuse into the wordmark's dots.

celebrate

Sorry

A request failed or hit the rate limit. He bows; the message itself says what happened.

sorry

Look here

On a dashboard he points to the item he is talking about, towards the end of the line.

point

Oh

Something unusual on the dashboard, or waking up.

surprised

Resting

Twenty quiet seconds on the launcher or the dashboard. Any movement wakes him.

sleep

Tap

A tap on him: the tile presses. Three quick taps fuse the eyes.

giggle, dizzy

Using him

Do

  • Let the state tell the truth: thinking only while a request is out, sorry only beside an error message.
  • Pair him with words. His face never replaces a message or a label.
  • Use Signal only on ink.

Don't

  • Add a mouth, limbs, an antenna, props or a glow.
  • Recolour the eyes or the tile, or set him on a gradient.
  • Stretch, outline or rotate the tile beyond his own moves.

Sizes

  • Launcher 56–64 px, chat header 40 px, messages 28 px, inline labels 18–24 px (compact), dashboard 96–184 px.
  • He keeps a clear space of a sixth of his size.
